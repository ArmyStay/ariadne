# Archived stills — before the third review

These PNGs were rendered from the build that existed **before** the third review
of the revised flow (2026-11-26). They are kept because they are the only renders
of some surfaces, and because a design argument is easier to check against a
before-picture than against a memory.

They are **not evidence**. Concretely, every frame in this folder predates:

- the register's bottom bar (a bar with no destination selected, announced as
  "Trips register, stacked from Home");
- the closed-trip matrix on Home, Capture, Record, Nightfall, Preferences and the
  bar's shutter;
- the single open exchange-rate editor in Preferences;
- the square plate everywhere (these show 4:5, 16:10, 16:9 and 1.4:1 in four
  different places);
- the 1:1 timeline rail with zone-correct hours and stacked hour collisions;
- the 44dp quiet-action target and the pillow-shaped shutter badge;
- the stacked New-trip dates and the stacked Nightfall decisions;
- the Trip card's single blue Share.

`docs/shots/` itself is empty of renders until `./tools/run-preview.sh --shots
docs/shots` is run against this build. When that happens, these files stay here
and the new ones land one directory up, so the two are never confused.
