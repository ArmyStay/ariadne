No red — it is not in the palette.
Serif for titles and totals; sans for running text.

WHAT CHANGED FROM v1
Material components gone entirely — no cards, no floating buttons, no elevation, no ripples.
Every screen is now a printed page with its own margins.
Six blurry routes became nine explicit pages.
The accent went from indigo to a single warm thread, used once per page.
Photos are inset on ruled field plates, framed like plates in a book.
